02 — QC Process

Three-Stage Inspection Protocol

Applied to every production order. Third-party inspection available through SGS, Bureau Veritas, and Intertek.

01

Pre-Production Sample Approval

Material samples, finish samples, and engineering drawings reviewed and approved before production authorization. Written approval required before production start.

02

In-Line Inspection at 30%

On-site inspection when production reaches 30% completion. Dimensional tolerance, finish quality, hardware function, and packaging verified. Non-conformities reported within 24 hours.

03

Final Random Inspection (AQL 2.5)

Pre-shipment inspection per ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 at Acceptable Quality Level 2.5. Container loading supervised. Seal number recorded. Report provided before payment release.

04 — Trade Compliance

Trade Compliance Approach

Kopperstone provides full documentation packages for every shipment entering the U.S. or Canada: commercial invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin, material safety data sheets, and applicable test reports.

Country-of-origin declarations are accurate and verifiable. AD/CVD exposure is assessed for each product category and disclosed to clients prior to order placement.

Commercial invoice with accurate declared value
Certificate of origin (factory-issued and notarized)
Material safety data sheets (MSDS/SDS)
Test reports for applicable certifications
ISF (Importer Security Filing) for U.S. entries

AD/CVD disclosure per product category
